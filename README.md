# Project-II---Data-Analytics----SalesDashboard
Data Analytics 


## Task 1: Data Cleaning and Preparation
Problem statement: The company’s sales dataset contains missing values, duplicate records, and unusual values. 
Clean the dataset to make it suitable for analysis.
	Requirements:
	1. Identify the total number of rows and columns.
	2. Find and remove duplicate records.
	3. Identify and handle missing values.
	4. Detect outliers in Quantity, Unit_Price, and Discount_Percent.
	5. Validate the Order_Date data type and check the consistency of sales and profit calculations.
	Expected output: A cleaned dataset and a data-quality summary.

## Task 2: Sales Performance Analysis
Problem statement: Management wants to understand the company's sales performance and identify its best-performing products and regions.
Requirements:
	1. Calculate total sales, total cost, total profit, and profit margin.
	2. Identify the top 10 products by sales revenue.
	3. Find the most profitable product category.
	4. Compare sales and profit across regions and cities.
	5. Analyze monthly sales trends and identify the best-performing month.
	Expected output: An analysis report with tables, charts, and key business insights.

## Task 3: Customer Analysis and Power BI Dashboard
Problem statement: Management wants an interactive dashboard to understand customer behavior, sales channels, and profitability.
Requirements:
	1. Analyze sales by Customer_Segment and identify the top 10 customers by sales.
	2. Compare sales and profit across sales channels and payment methods.
	3. Analyze the relationship between discount percentages and profit margins.
	4. Create KPI cards for Total Sales, Total Profit, Profit Margin, and Order Count.
	5. Build a Power BI dashboard with charts and slicers for Year, Region, Category, and Sales Channel.
	Expected output: An interactive Power BI dashboard and five actionable business recommendations.
